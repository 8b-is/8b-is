## 1 · the 16th Karmapa Guru Yoga — *Spontaneously Fulfilling All Desires*

Refuge and Bodhicitta · Visualization · the Seven-Branch Prayer · the mantra
**KARMAPA KHYENNO** · Absorption and blessing (the three lights of body, speech,
and mind dissolving the obscurations; the Guru melting into light).

## 2 · the Chenrezig sadhana — *For the Benefit of All Beings*

Visualizing Chenrezig (from **HRIH**, white, four arms, the antelope skin, the
Amitabha crown) · the praise (*Homage to Avalokiteshvara, Lord of Compassion*)
· the mantra **OM MANI PADME HUM** · dissolution and dedication.
