# music — Újonc (Závada Péter), Benedekelek

*"Újonc Péter — Benedekelek", uploaded 2010-11-30 by the channel Greg
Grice (462,140 views); the description dates the recording to **2007**.
The same radio as the Siska Finuccsi row above — the operator stayed on
the mix. The MC name is **Újonc**; the man behind it is **Závada Péter**,
the Hungarian poet, whose work with Akkezdet Phiai is where the alias
comes from. That attribution is corroborated by the video's own comment
thread rather than by the uploader, who is a third party — the row marks
it as such.*

> Mondj egy esti mesét mint Benedek Elek,
> Ha benned alszom el kérdés hogy benned-e kelek.
> Kérdés, hogy benned-e kelek.

and:

> Ha valóságot akarsz, olvass Móricz Zsigmondot
> Soha se nem mondtam, hogy mindig origit mondok.
