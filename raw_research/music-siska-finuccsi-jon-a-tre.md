## the honest note

The words are the song's, transcribed from the video's own description;
the Hungarian above is the artist's text and the English glosses are the
vault's reading, not an official translation. No claim is made about the
people named in the third verse. What the row records for the vault's
own bookkeeping is the coincidence of the morning: **Tatabánya is the
address on the application and the subject of the anthem**, and the
operator confirmed both in the same breath — the form field and the
chorus agree, which is the only kind of agreement this vault counts.
