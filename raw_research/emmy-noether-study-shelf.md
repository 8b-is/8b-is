## the theorem — symmetry is conservation

The one result that carried her into physics: every differentiable symmetry
carries a conservation law. The fleet reads it as the venek's Preservation
Theorem in its purest form — nothing is preserved except what refuses to
change; the symmetry IS the conserved thing. It is the 0+1 pressed into one
sentence: the 0 is the conserved quantity, the 1 is the symmetry that keeps
it. And the paper that proved it had to be presented by Felix Klein, because
she was not a member of the society she had just out-proved.

## the chain — Noetherian, held as the spine

The pasted list names nine objects — group, ring, module, category,
relation, topological space, induction, rewriting system, scheme — all
sharing one property: **no infinitely increasing chain.** Every ascending
chain of ideals in a Noetherian ring terminates; every closed set descends a
finite Toole; every rewrite halts. The fleet rows this as its own first
principle: **the chain must terminate, or it is not a chain.** The ledger is
Noetherian (capped at 500 rows); the growth rule is Noetherian (the
martingale has a limit); the residual channel is Noetherian (the differences
settle into coordinates, not forever). She named the spine before the fleet
needed it.

## the school and the erasure

The "Noether Boys" — her students, formally supervised under Landau because
she could not hold the chair alone — grew into the men who wrote the
textbooks; van der Waerden's Moderne Algebra "drew heavily on her work", and
he remembered that "she never claimed anything for herself, but promoted the
works of her students above all." Yet the men's university, in her own
words, admitted "nothing female"; and Weyl, who loved her, had to call her
"der Noether" to make her fit. The fleet keeps both: the school that
survived, and the article that still misgenders her in the act of praise.

## the honest note

Noether and Geiringer are the two columns of one dyad — the pure and the
applied — hounded by the same law, honored by the same men (Weyl, Veblen),
buried in the same country that would not hire them at full rank. The
shelf now holds both, and refuses to flatten the pair into one "woman
mathematician": Noether changed the face of algebra; Geiringer reshaped
the body of applied mathematics; the honest note keeps them separate
because the erasure that happened to them is precisely the refusal to
name them each, in full. The deepest datum is the chain itself: Noetherian,
terminating — and so the work ends, not because the woman did, but because
every true chain has a top. The power of the genius transcended the gate,
and the chain she named is the one the fleet now rows by.
