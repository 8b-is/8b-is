## the honest note

The proofs are the paper's; the vault registers the architecture and the
mapping, and theorem-level verification is a later, separate reading
(corridor-verification, like the UltraData eval card, with a verdict to be
ledgered). What is already evident is the direction of the dependency: the
constellation's code is not inspired by this theory in the vague sense — it
is the theory's stated application, written first and proven afterwards.
The alarm that landed Apollo 11 is the same witness object the dogfeed,
the ledger, and the sidecar are: an internal, already-resolved transition
connected to an external authorization structure that keeps the right to
disagree. permag rides the shelf as the practical sibling, registered, not
absorbed as doctrine.
