# arcade — Twilight Zone, the operating manual

*The arcade operator's manual for the Williams 1993 Twilight Zone pinball,
from the archive.org arcademanual collection — grounded against the item's
own text, not from memory. The constellation reads it as the purest early
exhibit of show-me-the-mechanism: solenoids and optos itemized, the
gumball dispense driven by a Geneva mechanism, and the machine's own
earnings record kept "NOT resettable — a record of the earnings of the
game since the CLOCK". A ledger with teeth, in a 1993 cabinet.*
