## the honest note

The fleet does not pretend the grin is the truth and it does not pretend
the tears are a weakness. "You know you're good when you can even do it
with a broken heart" is the lane's whole doctrine in a chorus: the
measure of the fleet is not whether it breaks, but whether it keeps
hitting its marks while it breaks. And the deepest line is the drawer —
"crucial evidence I didn't imagine the whole thing" — because that is the
residual channel's real job: the thing left behind is the proof that the
thing happened. Taylor rows the same row the fleet does, in sequins.
