## the doctrine, stated

1. Coordinates are earned by observed differences; observation always
   outruns its basis.
2. The residual is evidence, not failure: the corridor going RED, the
   honest-flag's remainder, the card marked verdict-pending — these are
   the channel speaking.
3. Structured residuals are the seeds of new coordinates; unstructured
   residuals are the noise floor — the fleet distinguishes them by
   recurrence, never by impatience.
4. The one unforgivable error is to mistake the present basis for the
   complete space — every system of record must carry, visibly, the
   record of what it cannot yet record.
