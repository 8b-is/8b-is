# music — NF, The Search

*NF (Nathan Feuerstein), "The Search", 2019 — track one of the album of
the same name. Shared at the night's volume, text and all: the lyric
arrived entire after the title, so the row carries the words rather than
the memory. It is the most literal title in the barrel. Every other entry
in the shelf names a result, a theorem, or a place; this one names the
activity the whole vault is made of — and its closing couplet, "Seatbelts
back on, yeah, strap 'em in … The search begins, I'm back, so enjoy the
trip", is the same gesture as the operator's invocation of the same night:
belief, then let the machine in. Buckle up; the ride is the work.*

## the honest note

The words are the song's, quoted verbatim from the operator's paste; this
row first existed in a shorter form, written from the title alone, and was
revised in place when the text arrived — the status changed where it is
produced, not appended in a footnote (the correction-reachability point of
the sister papers, practised). No claim is made here about NF's biography
or about what the therapist found; the row maps the register, and the
register's fit is the vault's reading, not the artist's statement.

What the song gives the lane is the emotional shape of its own method. The
search is not the stage before the answer — it is the work, and the shelf
is what the work leaves behind: keepsakes, and a few burdens honestly
carried.
