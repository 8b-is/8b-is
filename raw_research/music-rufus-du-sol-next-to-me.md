## the honest note

The lyrics, the director, the mechanism paragraph and every count above
are the **source's own**, taken from the official video page. Two
corrections, recorded rather than silently fixed, because the batch this
arrived in has a pattern:

  right next to me."** Those words are **not in the song**. The official

  retained here only as an example of an unmarked fabrication.
