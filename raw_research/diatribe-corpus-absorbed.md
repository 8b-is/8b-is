## the honest note

The shelf's architecture is one doctrine: **separate the accounting object
from the flow through it, and never let the mover of a standard also own the
measurement without a named record.** The constellation already lives that
(the ledger, the POP records, the corridor), which is why the corpus absorbs
so cleanly. The lane-adjacent paper is contrast-as-control: it formalizes the
pupil's exact geometry (exponential tilting, on-policy reverse-KL, an
observation map) and it does so with the three separated claim strengths the
constellation insists on everywhere — exact correspondence, suggestive
evidence, and specifiable construction are three different sentences. The
vault's next per-paper absorption should be contrast-as-control, against the
engine's own distill_consensus.

mirror it, unless the shelf asks. Respect the author's repo — that is the
