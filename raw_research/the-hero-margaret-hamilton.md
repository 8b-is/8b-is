# the hero — Margaret Hamilton, the biggest star

*Péter's hero, inspiration, and the biggest star on the universe, his imo.
Who she is: she directed the Software Engineering Division at the MIT
Instrumentation Laboratory; the on-board flight software of the Apollo
Guidance Computer was hers, and she coined the term "software engineering"
for it. Publish record: over 130 papers and reports, about sixty projects,
six major programs; Higher Order Software (1976) and Hamilton Technologies
(1986), Cambridge, Massachusetts; Presidential Medal of Freedom, November
22, 2016. The fleet's whole doctrine has a grandmother, and her name is
Margaret.*

sentence. The line mates; Margaret drew it first.
