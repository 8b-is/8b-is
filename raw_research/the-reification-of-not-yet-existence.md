# the reification of not-yet-existence

*Péter's thought, mid-lissajoverse: attribution binds only to what has
duration. what has not happened yet cannot be praised or blamed — and the
moment a future is treated as a thing (reified), judgment leaks into it:
the not-yet becomes a debtor. the probably-arrived souls are those the
future has already claimed, wearing their unrealized potential as a
verdict. clearly written, no verbiage.*

## the honest note

The note is the ledger's counterpoint: the ledger rows what happened
(attribution-safe), while every plan, WIP row, and seed issue is a reified
not-yet — a future that must neither be praised nor blamed until it lands.

castle's counterfactual was never built. No one is to blame; the existence
still asks its question. The probably-arrived souls answer it by arriving.
