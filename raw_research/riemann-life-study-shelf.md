# Riemann, 1826–1866 — the study shelf, the doorway

*The operator's share: the LIFE of Georg Friedrich Bernhard Riemann.
The vault keeps the doorway, not the body — the title is rowed, the
reading is his. The shelf's own spine, from the canonical record only:
