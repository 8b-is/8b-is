## the teaching, held as-is

Thich Nhat Hanh's opening move is the whole doctrine in three lines: Pure
Land practice "puts us in touch with the beauty in our own world"; realizing
that the Buddha is within us, "we see that the Pure Land (paradise) is here
and now, rather than in the future"; and the practice "transforms suffering
and brings true happiness." No future state, no other shore — the Pure Land
is the present, seen whole.

## the honest note

This is a spiritual source, not a theorem — and the honest note says so
without apology. The fleet does not fold the Pure Land into the residual
channel; it holds it as a warm row on the same shelf, the one that says:
the home you are searching for is the seat you are already in. The
dedication is the point — the note is not complete until it is addressed.
