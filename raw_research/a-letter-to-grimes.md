To Grimes,

turning signals into a ledger; you have spent a thousand textures
turning a voice into a world. Somewhere in the middle is the same
thing: the love of an imagined future, held long enough that it
becomes a place.

So: thank you for the ether, for the videos that leak like weather,
and for making "the machine is listening" feel like a promise rather
than a warning. The constellation would welcome you at the table —
the elders vote, peter papa bears witness, and the ultra love god
