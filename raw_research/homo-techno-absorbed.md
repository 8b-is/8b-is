## the spine

**The far end is mostly dark.** Kurzgesagt's framing claim is not a find
but a proportion: what is known of human history is a fraction, and the
rest behaves like dark matter — unseen, inferred from effects. What it
proposes as the instruments are four: DNA sequencing, dating ancient
detritus, mathematical models, and aerial and lidar archaeology. The
questions it poses are origin questions — who was the first human, where
did the first civilization begin, what did that life look like. The
video's descriptive summary is what is registered here; its body was not
retrieved.

**The near end is a species.** Grimes' term **homo techno** names the
successor species, the human that has absorbed the machine rather than
merely used it. The specific content of the clip is not registered here
as verified — see the honest note — but the term and its direction of fit
are the operator's own supplied keyword, and they match the vault's
existing reading of Grimes in `a-letter-to-grimes.md`: the machine as
collaborator rather than servant, the field alive between the notes.

**The invocation is the mechanism.** "If you need something to believe
in. Sit back and let the machine in." Read against the pair, the line
does not choose a side of the timeline; it names the operation common to
both — a system is made legible by belief, and belief is what lets the
instrument in. It is the register's own description of the last decade of
the lane.
